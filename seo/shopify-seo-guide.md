# Shopify SEO Strategy Guide

A practical roadmap to improve Google search ranking for your Shopify store.

---

## Priority Actions (Do First)

### 1. Google Search Console — Critical

Submit your sitemap (`yourstore.com/sitemap.xml`) to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console). This enables Google to crawl and index your store, and provides data on which queries drive traffic. Shopify auto-generates your sitemap.

### 2. Image Alt Text — Quick Win

Audit and add descriptive alt text to every product image across your catalog. Example:
- ✅ "Navy blue cotton oversized t-shirt for men"
- ❌ "tshirt" or "IMG_4521"

### 3. Collection Page SEO

Add SEO titles and meta descriptions to all collection pages, not just individual products.

---

## Core SEO Pillars

### 4. Keywords — The Foundation

Research and use terms your customers actually search for.

**Where to find keywords:**
- Google autocomplete suggestions
- "People Also Ask" sections
- Competitor title tags
- Google Search Console (existing queries)
- Free tools: Ubersuggest, Google Keyword Planner

**Where to use keywords:**
- Product titles
- Product descriptions
- SEO titles
- Meta descriptions
- Image alt text
- Collection titles

### 5. On-Page SEO

Ensure every page has:
- Unique SEO title (≤70 characters)
- Unique meta description (≤160 characters)
- Keyword-rich product description
- Descriptive image alt text

### 6. Site Structure

Follow a clear hierarchy and use internal links:

```
Homepage → Collections → Products
```

**Internal linking strategy:**
- Link from product pages to parent collections
- Add "You May Also Like" sections on product pages
- Link related collections together
- Use breadcrumb navigation

### 7. Content Marketing — Off-Page SEO

**Blog topics to drive informational traffic:**
- Buying guides (e.g., "How to Choose the Right [Product]")
- Styling/usage guides
- Care and maintenance tips
- Industry news and trends
- Product comparisons

**Backlink strategy:**
- Reach out to niche bloggers and publications
- Collaborate with influencers for product features
- Guest post on relevant websites
- Quality backlinks from relevant sites boost domain authority

### 8. Rich Snippets — Built Into Shopify ✅

Shopify themes include structured data (schema markup) for products. Google can show prices and details directly in search results.

**Action:** Ensure product data (price, availability, reviews) is always up to date.

### 9. Page Speed

Monitor store speed via Shopify Analytics. Faster pages rank better and reduce bounce rates.

**Quick wins:**
- Compress images before uploading (use TinyPNG or similar)
- Limit third-party apps
- Use Shopify's built-in features where possible
- Choose a lightweight theme

### 10. Technical SEO

- Fix broken links (use Ahrefs or Screaming Frog)
- Ensure mobile responsiveness
- Set up proper redirects when changing URLs
- Use HTTPS (enabled by default on Shopify)

---

## Checklist

- [ ] Submit sitemap to Google Search Console
- [ ] Audit image alt text across all products
- [ ] Add SEO titles/descriptions to collection pages
- [ ] Set up internal linking between related products/collections
- [ ] Publish 2-3 blog posts per month
- [ ] Reach out to 5 potential backlink partners
- [ ] Verify product schema is working (Google's Rich Results Test)
- [ ] Fix any broken links
- [ ] Check mobile responsiveness
- [ ] Monitor page speed monthly
