# SEO Strategy Overview — Naza Noor

A practical roadmap to improve Google search ranking for your Shopify store.

---

## Priority Actions (Do First)

### 1. Google Search Console — Critical

Submit your sitemap (`nazanoor.com/sitemap.xml`) to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console). This enables Google to crawl and index your store, and provides data on which queries drive traffic. Shopify auto-generates your sitemap.

### 2. Image Alt Text — Quick Win

Audit and add descriptive alt text to every product image across your catalog. Example:
- ✅ "Pure Modal Silk Azrak saree in red with mirror embroidery"
- ❌ "saree" or "IMG_4521"

### 3. Collection Page SEO

Add SEO titles and meta descriptions to all collection pages, not just individual products.

---

## Core SEO Pillars

### 4. Keywords — The Foundation

Research and use terms your customers actually search for:
- "handloom Dhakai Jamdani saree"
- "pure silk kaftan for Eid"
- "Banarasi silk suit"
- "handwoven cotton saree online"

Weave these naturally into:
- Product titles
- Product descriptions
- SEO titles
- Meta descriptions
- Image alt text

### 5. On-Page SEO — Already Done ✅

- SEO titles and meta descriptions on all 46 products — completed
- Continue monitoring for new products

### 6. Site Structure

Follow a clear hierarchy and use internal links:

```
Homepage → Collections → Products
```

**Internal linking strategy:**
- Link from kaftan product → kaftans collection
- Add "You May Also Like" sections on product pages
- Link related collections (e.g., "Sarees for Weddings" → "Wedding Collection")

### 7. Content Marketing — Off-Page SEO

**Blog topics to drive informational traffic:**
- Jamdani weaving heritage and history
- Styling guides (e.g., "How to Style a Jamdani Saree for Eid")
- Care tips for handloom fabrics
- Fabric comparison guides (cotton vs silk vs modal)

**Backlink strategy:**
- Reach out to fashion bloggers and South Asian lifestyle publications
- Collaborate with influencers for product features
- Quality backlinks from relevant sites boost domain authority

### 8. Rich Snippets — Already Built In ✅

Shopify themes include structured data (schema markup) for products. Google can show prices and details directly in search results. **Action:** Ensure product data (price, availability, reviews) is always up to date.

### 9. Page Speed

Monitor store speed via Shopify Analytics. Faster pages rank better and reduce bounce rates.

**Quick wins:**
- Compress images before uploading
- Limit third-party apps
- Use Shopify's built-in features where possible

---
