## The 10 Core Digital Marketing Strategies

### 1. Email Marketing & Behavioral Flows
- **Deep-Dive Guide:** [email-marketing-guide.md](../retention/email-marketing-guide.md)
- **Welcome Series:** Triggered when customer signs up without buying (3 emails over 7 days)
- **Abandoned Cart Sequence:** 3 emails at 1hr, 24hr, and 72hr intervals (recovers 10-30% of lost revenue)
- **Post-Purchase Flow:** Shipping confirmation, product education, review request
- **Browse Abandonment:** Triggered when customer views product 3+ times without adding to cart (6-12% conversion rate)
- **ROI:** Average Shopify store earns $36 for every $1 spent on email

### 2. SEO & Content Marketing
- **Deep-Dive Guide:** [seo-content-marketing-guide.md](../seo-content-marketing-guide.md)
- **Product Page SEO:** Title tags, meta descriptions, schema markup, internal linking
- **Blog Content:** 30-50 articles targeting buyer-intent and informational keywords
- **Benefits:** Compounds over time, zero CAC after content creation
- **Timeline:** Shows compounding results in 3-6 months

### 3. Paid Social Advertising
- **Deep-Dive Guide:** [paid-social-marketing-guide.md](../paid-social-marketing-guide.md)
- **Platforms:** Meta (Facebook/Instagram), TikTok, Pinterest, Snapchat, LinkedIn
- **Best Practices:**
  - Build after email flows and content are established
  - Optimize for "Purchase" or high-value conversion events
  - Use Shopify Audiences for 15-30% performance lift
- **Timeline:** Shows results in 30-60 days after creative testing

### 4. Organic Social Media
- Builds brand awareness and community
- Runs in parallel with paid efforts
- Platform-agnostic content strategy

### 5. SMS Marketing
- **Deep-Dive Guide:** [sms-marketing-guide.md](../retention/sms-marketing-guide.md)
- **ROI:** 12-30% click rates vs 3-5% on email
- **Caution:** Most permission-sensitive channel
- **Strategy:** Build after email flows mature
- **Risk:** One bad campaign can lose 15% of list

### 6. Search Engine Marketing (Google Ads)
- **Deep-Dive Guide:** [google-ads-marketing-guide.md](../google-ads-marketing-guide.md)
- **Google Shopping Ads:** Show product images, prices, and store name directly in search results
- **Best for:** Products with clear search demand
- **Priority:** Higher-intent clicks than standard search ads

### 7. Influencer & Affiliate Marketing
- **Shopify Collabs:** Partner with creators directly from Shopify admin
- **Benefits:** Scalable earned-media strategy
- **Stat:** 1 in 4 Americans discover new products through influencers

### 8. Content Marketing
- **Deep-Dive Guide:** [seo-content-marketing-guide.md](../seo-content-marketing-guide.md)
- Buying guides, how-tos, comparison articles
- Helps shoppers research before purchasing
- Pairs directly with SEO for organic discovery

### 9. Marketing Automation
- **Shopify Flow:** Automate campaigns based on customer segments
- **Shopify Sidekick:** AI-powered campaign creation and management
- **Lifecycle Automations:** Welcome, abandoned cart, win-back flows

### 10. Retention & Loyalty Programs
- **Customer Segmentation:** Personalized campaigns based on behavior
- **Post-Purchase Content:** 69% of shoppers feel more confident with follow-up content
- **Win-back Campaigns:** Re-engage inactive customers
- **VIP Programs:** Reward repeat customers

## Channel Build Order (Priority Sequence)

| Order | Channel | Why First |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| 1 | Email & Behavioral Flows | Captures existing customer behavior, highest ROI |
| 2 | SEO & Content | Compounds over time, zero CAC after creation |
| 3 | Paid Social | Amplifies channels 1 & 2 |
| 4 | Organic Social | Brand building, community |
| 5 | SMS | Highest per-send ROI, most permission-sensitive |

## Key Metrics to Track

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **AOV** | Average Order Value - amount customer spends per transaction |
| **CAC** | Customer Acquisition Cost - cost to acquire new customer |
| **Retention Rate** | Percentage of customers kept over time |
| **CLV** | Customer Lifetime Value - total spend over relationship |
| **ROAS** | Return on Ad Spend - revenue per dollar spent on ads |
| **Conversion Rate** | Benchmark: 2.74% average for ecommerce |

## Budget Guidelines

- **Recommended:** 10-25% of monthly revenue
- **Early Stage:** 15-20% of revenue
- **Mature Stores:** 7-15% of revenue
- **Below 10%:** Under-investing in growth
- **Above 20%:** Overpaying for acquisition

### Budget Allocation
- Paid Social: 50-70%
- Email Tools: 5-10%
- Content Production: 15-25%
- Tools/SaaS: 5-10%

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Starting with Paid Social Before Email** - Creates leaky bucket marketing
2. **Running 5 Channels at 20% Effort** - Master one channel before adding next
3. **Ignoring Shopify-Native Tools** - Missing 30-40% platform advantages
4. **Expecting Overnight Results** - Full strategy maturity takes 4-6 months
5. **Spreading Too Thin** - Focus on top 1-2 channels first
