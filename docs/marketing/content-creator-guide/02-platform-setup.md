## 2.1 Platform Selection Matrix

Choose your primary and secondary platforms based on your content style and niche:

| Platform | Best Content Style | Audience | Revenue Model | Creator Requirement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **TikTok** | Raw, authentic, trend-driven video | Gen Z / Millennial | Affiliate, LIVE gifts, TikTok Shop | On-camera comfort |
| **Instagram** | Polished lifestyle, Reels, carousels | 18–35, aspirational | Affiliate, brand deals, product tags | Visual storytelling |
| **YouTube** | Long-form reviews, tutorials, vlogs | Broad, all ages | AdSense, affiliate, YouTube Shopping | Production quality |
| **Pinterest** | Static pins, idea boards, product lifestyle | High-income, purchase intent | Affiliate links, boards | No face camera needed |
| **Blog/SEO** | Written guides, comparisons, reviews | High purchase intent | SEO affiliate, email list | Writing skill |

> [!TIP]
> **The Creator Stack:** Pick ONE primary platform to build your audience. Add a blog or YouTube for evergreen SEO. Use Pinterest as a passive traffic multiplier that costs almost no extra effort.

## 2.2 TikTok — Setup & Algorithm

### Setup Checklist

- [ ] Create a **TikTok Business or Creator Account**
- [ ] Apply to **TikTok Affiliate Marketplace** (requires 1K+ followers)
- [ ] Join **TikTok Shop Open Collaboration** (5K+ followers, 18+ years old) to promote any brand's products and earn commissions
- [ ] Set up **TikTok Series** to monetize exclusive content behind a paywall
- [ ] Link your YouTube or Instagram in bio for cross-platform traffic

### Algorithm Signals That Matter on TikTok (2026)

| Signal | Importance | Your Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Watch time / completion rate | 🔴 Critical | Hook viewers in first 1–2 seconds; strong endings |
| Shares & saves | 🔴 Critical | Create content worth saving or sending to a friend |
| Comments | 🟡 High | Ask direct questions; reply to early comments |
| Originality | 🟡 High | No watermarks from other platforms; native content only |
| Posting consistency | 🟠 Medium | 3–5 posts per week minimum during growth phase |

## 2.3 Instagram — Setup & Algorithm

### Setup Checklist

- [ ] Switch to a **Professional Account** (Business or Creator)
- [ ] Connect to a **Facebook Page** via Meta Accounts Center
- [ ] Install **Facebook & Instagram** app in your Shopify admin (if you sell products)
- [ ] Submit for **Instagram Shopping approval** (24–48 hours)
- [ ] Apply to **Instagram Creator Marketplace** for brand deal discovery

### Algorithm Signals That Matter on Instagram (2026)

| Signal | Weight | Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sends per Reach (DM Shares)** | Highest | Create content shareable enough to DM to a friend |
| **Saves** | High | Educational carousels, product breakdowns, tutorials |
| **Watch Time / Loop Rate** | High (Reels) | Strong hooks, concise storytelling, loop-worthy endings |
| **Originality** | High | No TikTok watermarks; native original content only |
| **SEO Keywords** | Rising | Keyword-rich captions over generic hashtag stacking |

## 2.4 YouTube — Setup

### Setup Checklist

- [ ] Create a **YouTube Brand Channel** (separate from personal account)
- [ ] Connect to **Google Merchant Center** via Google & YouTube channel (for YouTube Shopping)
- [ ] Enable **YouTube Shopping** (tag products directly in videos, Shorts, and livestreams)
- [ ] Apply to **YouTube Shopping Affiliate Program** (for Shopify Advanced/Plus stores)
- [ ] Set up a **YouTube Membership** for recurring community revenue

> [!TIP]
> YouTube videos rank on Google search for 2–5+ years. A well-researched comparison video made today will keep sending affiliate commissions for years. **This is your compound interest engine.**

## 2.5 Pinterest — Setup & Why It's Different

### Setup Checklist

- [ ] Create a **Pinterest Business Account** at business.pinterest.com
- [ ] Claim your website (DNS or HTML verification)
- [ ] Enable **Rich Pins** (pulls live price and stock from your Shopify store)
- [ ] Install the **official Pinterest Shopify app** for Hosted Checkout (in-app purchase, April 2026)
- [ ] Set up **Idea Pins** with affiliate product links

### Why Pinterest is a Creator's Secret Weapon

| Characteristic | Pinterest | Other Platforms |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Content Lifespan** | 4–12+ months (evergreen) | 24 hours to 7 days |
| **User Mindset** | Active purchase planning | Passive entertainment |
| **Creator Requirement** | No face camera needed | Usually requires on-camera |
| **Checkout** | In-app Hosted Checkout (April 2026) | Usually redirects externally |
| **Discovery** | Keyword search + Visual Lens | Algorithmic feed |
