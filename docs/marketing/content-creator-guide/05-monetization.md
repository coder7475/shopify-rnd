## 5.2 TikTok Shop Affiliate — Deep Dive

TikTok Affiliate is the fastest path from audience to income for product creators. **60–70% of TikTok Shop US GMV is affiliate-driven.**

### How It Works

```
You (Creator) → Tag product in video/LIVE → Viewer taps → Buys in-app
                → TikTok tracks attribution
                → You earn commission (typically 5–20%)
```

### Requirements

| Program | Follower Minimum | Age | Other |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| TikTok Affiliate Marketplace | 1,000+ | 18+ | Account in good standing |
| Open Collaboration (apply to brands) | 5,000+ | 18+ | Active posting history |
| Targeted Collaboration (brand invites you) | Varies | 18+ | Niche relevance |

### Affiliate Collaboration Types

| Type | Description | Best For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Open Collaboration** | Apply to any brand's open affiliate program | Growing creators building a portfolio |
| **Targeted Collaboration** | Brand directly invites you at higher commission rates | Niche authority creators |
| **Sample Requests** | Request free product to create authentic content | Building review content at zero cost |

### TikTok Affiliate Best Practices

- **Always disclose:** Add `#affiliate` or `#TikTokAffiliate` for affiliate content; `#ad` or `#sponsored` for paid partnerships.
- **Tag the product in-video** (not just bio link) — in-video product tags convert significantly higher than bio link clicks.
- **LIVE selling converts 30–40% higher** than standard videos. Go LIVE 3+ times per week if building serious affiliate revenue.
- **Authorize your best content for GMV Max:** Brands can run your affiliate content as a paid ad (with your permission) — this amplifies your reach and commissions simultaneously.
- **Niche down your product selection:** Promoting 2–3 highly relevant products consistently outperforms spraying dozens of unrelated products across your content.

### Commission Reference

| Category | Typical Commission Range |
| :--- | :--- |
| Fashion / Apparel | 8–15% |
| Beauty / Skincare | 10–20% |
| Electronics | 3–8% |
| Home & Kitchen | 5–12% |
| Sports & Outdoors | 6–12% |

## 5.3 Instagram Affiliate — Deep Dive

### Affiliate Tagging in Instagram Content

| Format | Tag Method | Tag Limit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Feed Posts** | Tag Products on share screen | 20 tags/post |
| **Stories** | Product Sticker from Sticker tray | 1 per slide |
| **Reels** | Tag Products on final share screen | 30 products/collections |

### Instagram Creator Marketplace

- Find brand deals directly in the Instagram app under **Professional Dashboard > Brand Deals**.
- Brands filter creators by niche, audience demographics, and engagement rate.
- Build a strong profile with consistent posting before applying — brands look at your last 30 days of content.
- Set your **creator rates** in Creator Marketplace so brands can book you without negotiation.

### 2026 Key Features

**Trial Reels:** Before committing a Reel permanently, A/B test it with non-follower audiences. High performers can then be boosted via Meta Ads Manager for significantly wider reach.

**Collab Posts:** Co-author a post with a brand or fellow creator — engagement is split across both profiles, doubling your exposure.

## 5.4 YouTube Shopping — Deep Dive

YouTube Shopping turns your videos into an always-on storefront:

- **Product Tagging in videos:** Tag products in video descriptions, timestamps, and Shorts. Viewers see a shopping cart icon during playback.
- **YouTube Shopping Affiliate Program** (Shopify Advanced/Plus stores): Earn commissions when your tagged products are purchased via your videos.
- **Livestream Shopping:** Tag products during live streams for real-time impulse purchasing.
- **Shorts:** Product tags in Shorts drive high-intent impulse clicks from mobile viewers.

> [!TIP]
> YouTube videos get indexed by Google within hours and can drive affiliate commissions **2–5 years after publication**. A strong review video is a long-term income asset — not a one-time post.

### YouTube Revenue Stacking

```
Single YouTube Video
├── AdSense Revenue (views × RPM)
├── Affiliate Commissions (product links in description)
├── YouTube Shopping Tags (in-video product clicks)
├── Brand Deal Integration (if sponsored)
└── Email List Growth (CTA to freebie/newsletter)
```

## 5.5 Pinterest Affiliate — Deep Dive

Pinterest is the only major platform where content organically lives for **4–12+ months**:

- Add affiliate links directly to your Pin URLs (no need for a middleman landing page).
- Create boards around specific buying intent: `"Minimalist Work Bags under $150"` not just `"Bags"`.
- Use the **Pinterest Trends Tool** (trends.pinterest.com) to find what's seasonally popular in your niche.
- Enable **Hosted Checkout** (via Shopify-Pinterest app) so viewers buy without leaving Pinterest — this also boosts your organic pin distribution.

**Pinterest Affiliate Pin Formula:**
```
Image: Product in aspirational lifestyle context (vertical, 1000×1500px)
Text overlay: "The only bag you need for [occasion]" (curiosity hook)
Title: "[Primary keyword] — [Product benefit]"
Description: 200–300 words with keyword phrases naturally woven in
Link: Direct affiliate URL with UTM tracking (?utm_source=pinterest)
Board: Specific niche board (e.g., "Minimalist Travel Carry-On Bags")
```

## 5.6 Brand Deal Pricing Guide

Rates vary by niche, engagement rate, and audience quality. Reference ranges for 2026:

| Platform | Follower Count | Estimated Rate Per Dedicated Post |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| TikTok | 10K–50K (micro) | $100–$500 |
| TikTok | 50K–200K (mid-tier) | $500–$2,500 |
| TikTok | 200K–1M (macro) | $2,500–$15,000 |
| Instagram Reel | 10K–50K | $150–$700 |
| Instagram Reel | 50K–200K | $700–$3,500 |
| YouTube (30-sec integration) | 50K–200K | $500–$5,000 |
| YouTube (dedicated video) | 50K–200K | $2,000–$15,000 |

> [!NOTE]
> **Engagement rate matters more than follower count.** A creator with 30K followers and 8% engagement rate is more valuable to brands than a 200K creator with 1% engagement. Always lead with engagement stats in your pitch.

### Pricing Frameworks

**Formula 1 — CPM-Based:**
`Rate = (Avg Views / 1,000) × CPM Rate`
CPM rates range from $20–$100+ depending on niche (finance/tech = higher, general lifestyle = lower).

**Formula 2 — Flat + Usage Rights:**
`Rate = Base Post Fee + Usage Rights Fee (typically 20–50% of base fee per month of usage)`

## 5.7 Media Kit — What You Need

Your media kit is your pitch document. Keep it to 1–2 pages and update it monthly.

**Your Media Kit Must Include:**
- [ ] Profile overview: niche, content pillars, audience description (1 sentence each)
- [ ] Average views per post (last 30 days) — screenshot from native analytics
- [ ] Engagement rate: `(likes + comments + saves) / reach × 100`
- [ ] Audience demographics: age breakdown, gender split, top 3 countries
- [ ] Top-performing content examples (3–5 posts with metrics)
- [ ] Rate card: flat fee, usage rights pricing, package deals
- [ ] Previous brand partnership examples (logos + results if available)
- [ ] Contact details and preferred booking method
