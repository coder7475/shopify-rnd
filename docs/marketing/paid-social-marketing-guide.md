## The State of Paid Social

For US-based Shopify SMB and DTC merchants, paid social remains the most scalable acquisition channel. However, privacy changes (like iOS 14.5) and the rise of machine learning have fundamentally shifted how media buying works. Today, success relies less on manual, granular media buying and entirely on **creative strategy** and **broad audience targeting**.

**Key statistic:** Over 70% of performance outcomes in paid social are driven entirely by the creative assets ([Meta Business Insights](https://www.facebook.com/business/insights)).

---

## Platform Allocation: Meta vs. TikTok

For the majority of SMB and DTC Shopify brands, budget should not be spread too thin across every available network. We recommend a focused allocation:

- **70% Meta (Facebook & Instagram):** Still the most robust machine learning algorithm and the primary volume driver for DTC brands. It offers stable, scalable performance across all demographics.
- **30% TikTok:** Crucial for reaching Gen Z and Millennials, and for capitalizing on viral, entertainment-first commerce.

*Note: Avoid diversifying into 4-5 platforms until you are spending upwards of $50k-$100k/month profitably on Meta and TikTok.*

## Meta Strategy: Consolidated Account Structures

The days of hundreds of ad sets targeting lookalikes, interests, and precise demographics are over. Today, the Meta algorithm requires data liquidity (volume of conversions) to optimize effectively.

### Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns (ASC)

**Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns (ASC)** use machine learning to automate targeting, placement, and budget optimization. This is the cornerstone of modern Meta strategy.

- **Broad Targeting:** Let the algorithm find your buyers. Restrict targeting only by country (e.g., US) and perhaps age/gender if strictly necessary for the product.
- **Consolidation:** Aim for 1-3 core campaigns (e.g., one ASC for acquisition, one standard campaign for specific testing). Consolidating budget into fewer ad sets exits the "learning phase" faster.

*"The algorithm is smarter than you. Feed it great creative and broad audiences, and it will find your customers at the best CPA."* — Industry Standard Best Practice.

## Creative Strategy: The New Targeting

With broad targeting being the standard, **"creative is the new targeting."** Your video or image dictates who stops scrolling and who clicks. 

### Core Formats
- **User-Generated Content (UGC):** Authentic, relatable content from real people using your product.
- **TikTok-Style Lo-Fi Video:** Shot on an iPhone, unpolished, native to the platform.
- **Direct Response (DR) Hooks:** The first 3 seconds must grab attention, state a problem, or present an irresistible visual.

### Modular Framework for Creative Testing

Don't test entirely new videos every time. Use a **modular testing framework**:
1. **The Hook (0-3s):** The most critical part. Test 3-5 different hooks (visuals, text overlays, opening lines) with the same body.
2. **The Body (3-15s):** The core selling proposition, benefits, and social proof.
3. **The Call to Action (CTA):** What the user should do next ("Shop Now", "Get 20% Off").

By testing hooks against a proven body, you isolate variables and scale winners predictably.

## TikTok Strategy: Entertainment First

TikTok requires a fundamentally different creative approach than legacy Facebook ads. On TikTok, ads must not look like ads.

- **Native Feel:** Use native text fonts, trending audio (when commercially licensed), and fast-paced editing.
- **Creator-Led:** Partner with micro-influencers to create content for your Spark Ads.
- **Direct Response:** While entertaining, ensure the product value proposition is crystal clear within the first 5 seconds.

## Other Platforms: Pinterest & Snapchat

While Meta and TikTok dominate, other platforms can be tested for specific niches:

- **Pinterest:** Excellent for highly visual categories (home decor, fashion, wedding). High intent, but longer sales cycles. Use for top-of-funnel discovery.
- **Snapchat:** Good for very young demographics (Gen Z) and AR lens experiences, but generally yields lower direct-response ROAS compared to Meta/TikTok for SMBs.

## Measuring Success & ROI

For DTC brands, move away from looking at platform-reported ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) in a vacuum due to attribution window limitations.

### Key Metrics to Track
| Metric | What It Measures | Target |
|--------|-----------------|--------|
| **CPA (Cost Per Acquisition)** | Cost to acquire a new customer | Must be lower than your gross margin |
| **MER (Marketing Efficiency Ratio)** | Total Revenue ÷ Total Ad Spend | 3.0+ (Blended across all channels) |
| **Hook Rate** | % of people who watch the first 3s | 25%+ |
| **Hold Rate** | % of people who watch the whole video | Depends on length, generally >10% |
| **Click-Through Rate (CTR)** | % who clicked the ad | 1%+ (Link CTR) |
