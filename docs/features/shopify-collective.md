# Shopify Collective — Complete Guide

## What is Shopify Collective?

Shopify Collective is a **free, Shopify-built app** that connects Shopify stores so they can sell each other's products — without holding any inventory. It enables a wholesale-style relationship between Shopify merchants, where one store (the **supplier**) shares products and another store (the **retailer**) imports and sells them.

There are no upfront costs, no inventory purchases, and no complex integrations. Everything runs inside Shopify.

---

## Why Use Shopify Collective?

| Benefit | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Zero inventory risk** | Retailers don't buy stock upfront. Products ship directly from the supplier to the customer. |
| **Expand product catalog** | Instantly add complementary products to your store without sourcing, negotiating, or warehousing. |
| **New sales channels** | Suppliers get access to a network of retailers who promote and sell their products. |
| **No extra marketing spend** | Suppliers reach new customers through retailers' existing traffic and audiences. |
| **Free to use** | No subscription fees for either retailers or suppliers on any Shopify plan. |
| **Automated order flow** | Orders forward to suppliers automatically, tracking syncs back, and payments settle through Shopify Payments. |
| **Both roles at once** | A single store can be both a retailer and a supplier simultaneously. |

## How It Works

### As a Retailer

1. **Discover products** — Browse the Collective Discovery page or receive invites from suppliers.
2. **Import products** — Instantly import products from public price lists, or request access to private price lists.
3. **Set your retail price** — The supplier sets a cost price (their price to you); you set the retail price on your store. Your profit is the difference (typically 20–50% margin).
4. **Customer orders** — A customer buys the product on your store at your retail price.
5. **Order forwards automatically** — The order is sent to the supplier for fulfillment.
6. **Supplier ships directly** — The supplier fulfills and ships the product straight to your customer.
7. **Tracking syncs** — Tracking numbers sync from the supplier back to your store, triggering your branded shipping notifications.

### As a Supplier

1. **Create price lists** — Add products to public or private price lists and set a retailer margin (minimum 5% for public lists).
2. **Share with retailers** — Public lists are discoverable by all eligible retailers. Private lists are shared with specific retailer connections.
3. **Retailers sell your products** — Retailers import your products and sell them on their stores at their chosen retail price.
4. **Receive orders** — When a retailer makes a sale, you receive the order in your Shopify admin.
5. **Fulfill and ship** — You fulfill the order and ship it directly to the end customer.
6. **Get paid** — The retailer pays you the cost price automatically through Shopify Payments when you mark the order as fulfilled.

## When to Use Shopify Collective

### Use as a Retailer when you want to:

- **Test new product categories** without committing to inventory (e.g., add jewelry to complement your clothing line).
- **Fill gaps in your catalog** quickly for seasonal or trending products.
- **Launch a store faster** by curating products from established brands.
- **Run a dropship-style model** without needing third-party dropshipping apps.

### Use as a Supplier when you want to:

- **Expand distribution** — Get your products in front of customers through other stores' traffic.
- **Reduce marketing costs** — Let retailers promote your products to their audiences.
- **Scale without new channels** — Use other Shopify stores as additional sales channels.
- **Maintain control** — You set the cost price, manage fulfillment, and control how your brand is represented through pricing.

## Eligibility & Requirements

Both retailers and suppliers must meet these requirements:

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| **Active Shopify plan** | Store must be on a paid plan (not Pause and Build). |
| **Supported country** | Store must be based in one of 37 supported countries (US, UK, Canada, Australia, EU countries, Japan, Singapore, etc.). |
| **Supported currency** | Store must use one of 18 supported currencies (USD, GBP, EUR, CAD, AUD, JPY, etc.). |
| **Shopify Payments active** | Account must be set up with active payouts. Payout currency must match store currency. |
| **Tax compliance** | UK/EU stores must use Shopify Tax, Basic Tax, or a third-party tax app (not Manual Tax). VAT/GST verification required. Canadian stores must verify GST/HST. |
| **Network Intelligence** | Must be enabled in privacy settings. |
| **ID verification** | May be required (ID document + selfie check). Skipped if already completed for Shopify Payments or Plus upgrade. |
| **Terms compliance** | Must accept Collective's Terms of Service, Shopify's Terms of Service, and Acceptable Use Policy. |

### Supported Countries (37)

Australia, Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Canada, Croatia, Cyprus, Czechia, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, Germany, Gibraltar, Greece, Hong Kong, Hungary, Ireland, Italy, Japan, Latvia, Liechtenstein, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Mexico, Netherlands, New Zealand, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Singapore, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, United Kingdom, United States.

### Supported Currencies (18)

AUD, GBP, CAD, CZK, DKK, EUR, HKD, HUF, JPY, MXN, NZD, NOK, PLN, RON, SGD, SEK, CHF, USD.

> **Important:** To connect with a supplier/retailer, both stores must be in the **same country** and use the **same currency**.

## Getting Started

### As a Retailer

1. Install [Shopify Collective](https://apps.shopify.com/collective) from the App Store.
2. Enable **Shopify Network Intelligence** in your privacy settings.
3. Complete ID verification if prompted.
4. Go to **Discovery** to browse suppliers and products.
5. Choose how to connect:
   - **Instant Import** — Import products from public price lists immediately (no approval needed).
   - **Request Pricing** — Send a message to a supplier requesting access to their private price lists.
   - **Invite by Email** — Invite a specific supplier using their email address.
   - **Signup Link** — Share your unique signup link with suppliers to auto-connect.
6. Import products, set your retail prices, and publish.

### As a Supplier

1. Install [Shopify Collective: Supplier](https://apps.shopify.com/collective-supplier) from the App Store.
2. Enable **Shopify Network Intelligence** in your privacy settings.
3. Complete ID verification if prompted.
4. Create a **Price List**:
   - Choose **Public access** (any eligible retailer can import) or **Private access** (specific retailers only).
   - Set your **retailer margin** (minimum 5% for public lists, recommended 20–50%).
   - Add products to the price list.
   - Set status to **Active**.
5. Share the price list — retailers will discover and import your products.
6. When orders come in, fulfill and ship them like any other order.

## Key Settings & Configuration

### Price Lists (Supplier)

| Setting | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Public vs Private** | Public = discoverable by all eligible retailers. Private = shared with specific retailers only. |
| **Retailer margin** | Percentage deducted from retail price to calculate cost price. Minimum 5% for public lists. Recommended: 20–50%. |
| **Status** | **Active** = products available for import. **Draft** = hidden from retailers. |
| **Product sync** | Cost price and inventory sync automatically. Retail price, compare-at price, images, description, variants, SKU, barcode, HS code, and origin sync on import. |

### What Syncs vs What Doesn't

| Syncs Automatically | Doesn't Sync |
|---------------------|--------------|
| Cost price | Collections |
| Inventory | Tags |
| Retail price (if enabled by retailer) | Discount codes |
| Compare-at price (if enabled by retailer) | Custom metafields |
| Product title, images, description | |
| Variants, SKU, barcode | |
| HS code, country of origin | |

### Shipping (Retailer)

- A default **Shopify Collective shipping profile** is created automatically.
- Supplier shipping rates (flat rate, calculated, or manual) pass through to your checkout.
- You can override supplier rates with **custom shipping profiles** or **free shipping discounts**.
- **Split shipping** in checkout lets customers see per-shipment costs when items come from multiple suppliers.

### Shipping (Supplier)

- Set flat rates, calculated rates, or manual invoicing per product and shipping zone.
- Flat and calculated rates are paid automatically when both parties have automatic payments enabled.
- Manual invoicing shows as "Free Shipping" at checkout — coordinate payment outside Collective.

### Payments (Retailer)

- **Automatic payments** are enabled by default when both parties meet eligibility.
- Cost price + shipping is debited from your Shopify Payments balance when the supplier fulfills.
- No extra fees for using Collective's payment system.
- If funds are insufficient, Collective retries automatically. Adjust payout schedule to weekly/monthly to maintain balance.

### Payments (Supplier)

- Payment is received in your Shopify Payments account when you mark the order as fulfilled.
- Refunds are automatically transferred back to the retailer when both have automatic payments on.

### Policies (Retailer)

- Configure how you handle product imports (automatic vs manual), shipping rate display, and returns.
- Set return rules that sync with your supplier's admin.

## Limitations & Incompatibilities

### Not Supported

| Feature/App | Reason |
|-------------|--------|
| **Digital products** | Collective only supports physical products. |
| **Gift cards** | Not supported. |
| **Pause and Build plan** | Must be on an active paid plan. |
| **Managed Markets** | Incompatible with Collective. |
| **Pickup in store** | Collective products ship from supplier only. |
| **Metafields** | Don't sync from supplier to retailer (retailers can use their own metafields). |
| **Subscriptions** | Not supported on supplier side (retailers can use subscriptions on imported products). |
| **Combined listings** | Not supported on supplier side. |
| **Product bundles** | Don't sync from supplier to retailer (retailers can create bundles on their store). |
| **International duties/taxes** | Not passed from retailer to supplier on shipping labels. |

### Compatible Features

- Product bundles (retailer side)
- Subscriptions (retailer side)
- Combined listings (retailer side)
- Shopify Flow
- Shopify POS (in-store purchase possible, but items still ship from supplier)

## Best Practices

### For Retailers

- **Start with instant imports** from public price lists to test products before committing.
- **Set competitive retail prices** — your margin is the difference between retail and cost price.
- **Keep sufficient Shopify Payments balance** — set payouts to weekly instead of daily to avoid insufficient funds.
- **Review supplier profiles** — check ratings, reviews, verified tracking badges, and shipping origins.
- **Communicate with suppliers** — discuss pricing, shipping expectations, and return handling.

### For Suppliers

- **Offer attractive margins** — 20–50% incentivizes retailers to promote your products.
- **Use public price lists** for broad distribution; private lists for curated partnerships.
- **Ship promptly** — retailers and their customers rely on quick fulfillment.
- **Monitor your shipping performance** — track metrics to maintain good retailer relationships.
- **Keep inventory accurate** — out-of-stock products can still sell if "Continue selling when out of stock" is enabled, but this creates fulfillment issues.
- **Communicate proactively** — notify retailers about stock issues, delays, or pricing changes.
