> **Target Audience:** Small D2C / fashion stores on the **Basic plan**  
> **Verified:** 5 Oct 2026, against the live Shopify App Store listings

---

## 📌 How to Read This Doc

- **Pricing** and **Requirements** are quoted from each app's App Store listing. If the listing says nothing, the field reads *"Not stated on listing"*.
- **Basic?** answers "Can a Basic-plan store use this?"
  - ✅ **Yes**: the listing has no plan restriction.
  - ⚠️ **Partly**: some features need Plus or another condition.
  - ❌ **No**: the listing says it's Plus-only.
- **Verdict** is a recommendation for a small D2C or fashion store on Basic.
- Ratings and review counts were taken on the verification date and will change.

> [!NOTE]
> Most Shopify listings don't list plan requirements at all. ✅ means "no restriction stated", not "officially confirmed for Basic". If an app doesn't work after you install it, check the app's help docs.

## 2. Marketing & Customer Growth

### 1. Shopify Forms
- **Summary:** Inline and pop-up forms that collect subscriber signups, wholesale leads and business information. Forms can match your branding, offer discounts and be translated.
- **Pricing:** Free
- **Requirements:** Not stated on listing. Works with: Shopify Admin, Shopify Email.
- **Use cases:**
  - A newsletter pop-up offering "10% off your first order".
  - A wholesale or stockist enquiry form for boutiques that want to carry your brand.
  - Collecting preferences (for example sizes or style) into customer metafields for segmentation.
- **Basic?** ✅ **Verdict:** Install. It's the base of your email list.

### 2. Shop
- **Summary:** Lists your brand on the Shop app. The listing says "you pay no marketplace fees to sell on Shop". It supports video, custom headers, collections, subscriptions, offers, 1-tap Shop Pay checkout and abandoned-cart push notifications.
- **Pricing:** Free
- **Requirements:** Not stated on listing. Works with: Shop Pay, Shop Promise, Shop Minis, Shopify Flow.
- **Use cases:**
  - Free discovery by Shop app users browsing new brands.
  - Faster repeat purchases through 1-tap Shop Pay checkout.
  - Automatic cart-recovery push notifications.
- **Basic?** ✅ **Verdict:** Install.

### 9. Shopify Collabs
- **Summary:** Affiliate and creator marketing: a custom application page, direct invites, product gifting, personalised links and codes, and automatic commission payouts through your Shopify bill.
- **Pricing:** "Free to install. + 2.9% commission processing fee on automatic payments may apply."
- **Requirements:** Not stated on listing.
- **Use cases:**
  - Gifting new-season pieces to micro-influencers and tracking the sales they bring in.
  - Running an ambassador program with unique discount codes.
- **Basic?** ✅ **Verdict:** Recommended for fashion brands.

### 15. Shopify Messaging
- **Summary:** Email, SMS and WhatsApp campaigns and automations from inside Shopify, with templates, Sidekick-assisted email editing and performance tracking.
- **Pricing:** "Your first 10,000 emails each month are free. After, pricing starts at $1/1000 emails per month." (The listing gives no SMS or WhatsApp rates.)
- **Requirements:** Not stated on listing. Works with: Shopify Flow, Shopify Forms.
- **Use cases:**
  - New-arrival and sale announcement emails.
  - Abandoned-checkout and welcome automations.
  - Order or promo updates over WhatsApp, where customers prefer it.
- **Basic?** ✅ **Verdict:** Install. 10,000 free emails a month covers most small stores.

### 24. Shopify Product Network
- **Summary:** Shows products from other brands on your store as an "instant catalog". You earn commission in cash or ad credits when customers buy them, with no stocking or shipping on your side.
- **Pricing:** Free
- **Requirements:** Not stated on listing.
- **Use cases:**
  - Filling gaps in your range (for example accessories next to your clothing) without holding inventory.
- **Basic?** ✅ **Verdict:** Optional. Third-party products appear on your storefront, so check that they fit your brand.

### 28. Shopcodes
- **Summary:** QR codes that link to products on your store, with scan and purchase tracking in Shopify Analytics.
- **Pricing:** Free
- **Requirements:** Not stated on listing.
- **Use cases:**
  - QR codes on hang tags, packaging inserts or flyers.
  - Pop-up or market stall signage linking to a product.
- **Basic?** ✅ **Verdict:** Recommended.

## 3. Merchandising, Pricing & Selling

### 6. Shopify Smart Pricing
- **Summary:** AI price tips (markdowns for slow sellers, markups for fast sellers) based on your sales, inventory, costs and seasonality. Price tips for new products are in early access. "Select US merchants can also A/B test prices."
- **Pricing:** Free
- **Requirements:** Not stated on listing. A/B testing is limited to select US merchants.
- **Use cases:**
  - Deciding end-of-season markdowns.
  - Pricing a new product against similar ones sold on Shopify.
- **Basic?** ✅ **Verdict:** Recommended.

### 8. Shopify Subscriptions
- **Summary:** Auto-billed subscriptions (weekly, monthly or yearly) with discounts. Customers can cancel, skip or pause. Includes email templates and reporting, and also works on POS.
- **Pricing:** Free
- **Requirements:** Not stated on listing.
- **Use cases:**
  - Basics (socks, underwear) or consumables on a "subscribe & save" model.
- **Basic?** ✅ **Verdict:** Only if you have repeat-purchase products.

### 14. Shopify Bundles
- **Summary:** Fixed bundles and multipacks created in the admin. Customers can choose variants (size, colour) for each item, and inventory updates in real time.
- **Pricing:** Free
- **Requirements:** Not stated on listing.
- **Use cases:**
  - "Complete the look" outfit bundles.
  - 3-pack multipacks at a discount.
- **Basic?** ✅ **Verdict:** Recommended for raising average order value.

### 18. Shopify Digital Products
- **Summary:** Attach files or links to products, set access limits and send access links after purchase. Digital files can be combined with physical variants.
- **Pricing:** Free
- **Requirements:** Not stated on listing.
- **Use cases:**
  - Selling digital style guides or lookbooks.
  - Adding a digital extra to a physical product.
- **Basic?** ✅ **Verdict:** Only if you sell digital goods.

### 23. Buy Button channel
- **Summary:** Embeddable buy buttons for any website or blog, plus quick checkout links you can paste into email, direct messages or social posts.
- **Pricing:** Free
- **Requirements:** Not stated on listing.
- **Use cases:**
  - Sending a direct checkout link to a customer who asked about a product on Instagram or WhatsApp.
  - Buy buttons on a blog or link-in-bio page.
- **Basic?** ✅ **Verdict:** Recommended. Checkout links are very useful for social selling.

## 4. Storefront, Search & Checkout

### 5. Shopify Checkout Blocks
- **Summary:** "Customize your thank you and order status pages. Add content blocks and order value limits." On Plus, the same customizations extend to the full checkout.
- **Pricing:** Free
- **Requirements (from listing):**
  - All plans: content blocks on the Thank you and Order status pages, order value limits and address format rules.
  - "Shopify Plus: Create custom Order & Shipping discounts"
  - "Shopify Plus: Customize checkout with content blocks, custom fields, & more"
  - "Shopify Plus: Hide, rename, & reorder delivery and payment methods"
- **Use cases (Basic):**
  - A "follow us on Instagram" or care-instructions block on the Thank you page.
  - Minimum or maximum order value limits.
- **Basic?** ⚠️ Partly. **Verdict:** Install, but expect only the post-purchase pages.

### 11. Shopify Search & Discovery
- **Summary:** Custom filters, synonym groups, product boosts, related and complementary product recommendations, and search analytics.
- **Pricing:** Free
- **Requirements:** Not stated on listing.
- **Use cases:**
  - Filters for size, colour, fabric and fit on collection pages.
  - Synonyms (for example "kurti" ↔ "tunic") so searches don't return zero results.
  - "Pairs well with" recommendations on product pages.
- **Basic?** ✅ **Verdict:** Install. See also [search_and_discovery.md](file:///home/fahad/Projects/shopify/guides/search_and_discovery.md).

### 16. Shopify SimGym
- **Summary:** AI shoppers with human-like personas simulate buyer behaviour on your storefront, so you can test changes before they go live. "Currently in AI Research Preview."
- **Pricing:** "Free to install. Charges per simulation run. More details available in the app."
- **Requirements:** Not stated on listing.
- **Use cases:**
  - Testing a theme redesign or campaign layout for add-to-cart and navigation issues.
- **Basic?** ✅ **Verdict:** Optional. It's a preview feature and each run costs money.

### 20. Shopify Translate & Adapt
- **Summary:** "Auto-translate up to two languages for free, and manually add as many translations as you wish." Includes a side-by-side editor and adapting content between markets that share a language.
- **Pricing:** Free
- **Requirements:** Not stated on listing.
- **Use cases:**
  - A second language for a local or diaspora audience (for example English + Bangla).
- **Basic?** ✅ **Verdict:** Install if you sell in more than one language.

## 5. Automation, AI & Support

### 3. Shopify Flow
- **Summary:** "Automation and integration platform for ecommerce." Hundreds of templates, custom logic, and integrations with tags, metafields, Slack, Google Sheets and more.
- **Pricing:** Free
- **Requirements:** Not stated on listing.
- **Use cases:**
  - Hide sold-out products and show them again when restocked.
  - Tag high-value customers as VIP.
  - Low-stock alerts sent to email or Slack.
- **Basic?** ✅ **Verdict:** Install.

### 4. Shopify Inbox
- **Summary:** "An AI sales associate for your online store." It learns from your catalog, policies and store content, and personalises replies for shoppers signed in with Shop. You can run it with or without the AI agent.
- **Pricing:** Free
- **Requirements:** Not stated on listing.
- **Use cases:**
  - 24/7 answers to sizing and delivery questions.
  - Live chat to help hesitant shoppers.
- **Basic?** ✅ **Verdict:** Install. See [shopify_inbox_guide.md](file:///home/fahad/Projects/shopify/guides/shopify_inbox_guide.md).

### 13. Shopify Knowledge Base
- **Summary:** "View and customize the FAQs that AI shopping agents use to answer questions about your store." Also shows how often AI agents request your store's information.
- **Pricing:** Free
- **Requirements:** Not stated on listing.
- **Use cases:**
  - Making sure AI shopping agents describe your returns, delivery and sizing correctly.
- **Basic?** ✅ **Verdict:** Recommended. It takes very little effort.

### 25. Fraud Control
- **Summary:** Fraud risk reports and analytics, recommended or custom rules that "block checkouts before they become orders", and automation through Flow.
- **Pricing:** Free
- **Requirements:** Not stated on listing.
- **Use cases:**
  - Blocking repeat fraudsters before they place orders.
- **Basic?** ✅ **Verdict:** Recommended. Note the low rating (2.2), so review the rules it blocks.

## 6. Sourcing & Sales Channels

### 29. Shopify Collective (retailer)
- **Summary:** Import products from "eligible brands on Shopify". Suppliers ship the orders, and inventory and pricing sync in real time.
- **Pricing:** Free ("No setup fees, no high commissions")
- **Requirements:** Only "eligible brands" are mentioned. The listing doesn't state merchant eligibility (country, currency). See [shopify-collective.md](file:///home/fahad/Projects/shopify/guides/shopify-collective.md).
- **Use cases:**
  - Adding accessories or footwear without buying stock.
- **Basic?** ✅ **Verdict:** Optional. Check eligibility in the app.

### 7. Shopify Collective: Supplier
- **Summary:** Share selected products with retailers using custom price lists. Retailers' orders sync to your store and you get paid when you ship.
- **Pricing:** Free
- **Requirements:** Not stated on listing.
- **Use cases:**
  - Wholesale distribution through other Shopify boutiques.
- **Basic?** ✅ **Verdict:** Only if you want retailers to sell your products.

### 12. Shopify Marketplace Connect
- **Summary:** Sell on Amazon, Target Plus, eBay and Walmart. Listings, orders and inventory are managed in Shopify. Formerly Codisto.
- **Pricing:** "First 50 marketplace-synced orders/mo free, 1% fee per additional synced-order, capped at $99/month."
- **Requirements:** Not stated on listing. You need your own marketplace seller accounts.
- **Basic?** ✅ **Verdict:** Only if you sell on these marketplaces.

### 35. Sell on WordPress
- **Summary:** A sales channel that adds Shopify products to WordPress pages and syncs products, orders and inventory.
- **Pricing:** Free
- **Requirements:** Not stated on listing. Needs a WordPress site.
- **Basic?** ✅ **Verdict:** Only if you have a WordPress site. Low rating (1.7).

## 7. Retail / POS

### 39. Point of Sale
- **Summary:** In-person selling with unified customers, orders and inventory, local pickup, gift cards, split payments, returns and exchanges.
- **Pricing (from listing):**
  - **POS Lite: Free.** "Included with all Shopify plans. Best for selling at pop-ups, markets and fairs."
  - **POS Pro: $89/month per location.** 14-day free trial. "Best for selling at brick-and-mortar stores."
- **Basic?** ✅ **Verdict:** Install POS Lite if you sell at pop-ups or markets. See [shopify_pos_guide.md](file:///home/fahad/Projects/shopify/guides/shopify_pos_guide.md).

### 21. Retail Barcode Labels
- **Summary:** Generates Code-128 barcodes and prints labels (price, SKU, variant) on Avery, Dymo or Zebra labels using any installed printer.
- **Pricing:** Free
- **Basic?** ✅ **Verdict:** Install if you scan products at POS.

### 31. Shopify Counter
- **Summary:** "Connect your store to Shopify Counter" to display your sales and orders on the Counter.
- **Pricing:** Free
- **Requirements:** You need a Shopify Counter. The listing doesn't say how to get one.
- **Basic?** ✅ **Verdict:** Only if you already own a Counter.

### 37. Shopify TSE (KassenSichV)
- **Summary:** A cloud TSE (technical security system) for Germany's KassenSichV cash-register rules. Records and exports POS transactions.
- **Pricing:** "$9/month. Monthly price is per location."
- **Requirements:** German retailers using Shopify POS.
- **Basic?** ✅ (Germany only) **Verdict:** Ignore unless you run POS in Germany.

## 8. Orders, Fulfillment & Shipping

### 10. Shopify Order Printer
- **Summary:** Pick lists, packing slips, invoices and receipts. Customer-facing templates can be fully edited with HTML, CSS and Liquid, and you can print up to 50 orders in bulk. "Customizing POS printer receipts is not supported."
- **Pricing:** Free
- **Basic?** ✅ **Verdict:** Install.

### 33. Shopify Fulfillment Network
- **Summary:** Connects you with third-party logistics providers (Flexport, ShipBob, Shipfusion, ShipMonk) offering 2- and 3-day delivery.
- **Pricing:** "Free to install. No up-front costs. End-to-end fulfillment priced per item. Each product is priced by weight."
- **Requirements:** Not stated on listing. Coverage depends on the partners.
- **Basic?** ✅ **Verdict:** Consider once you outgrow shipping orders yourself.

### 40. Shopify Planet
- **Summary:** Carbon-neutral shipping funded through carbon removal projects, with badges and widgets. You, your customers or both can pay.
- **Pricing:** "3 subscription options ranging from $0.00 to $0.15 per order."
- **Basic?** ✅ **Verdict:** Optional. Worth it if sustainability is part of your brand.

## 10. Finance, Tax & Migration

### 22. Shopify Bill Pay
- **Summary:** Business bill payments "powered by Melio". Pay from Shopify Balance, a card or ACH, with batch and recurring payments. Syncs with Gmail, QuickBooks Online and Stocky.
- **Pricing:** "Free to install. Transaction fees may apply. Transaction fees are billed by Melio separately."
- **Requirements:** Not stated. The listing mentions ACH and Shopify Balance, which points to US merchants.
- **Basic?** ✅* **Verdict:** Optional, and probably US-only.

### 38. Shopify Data Exporter ‑ Tax
- **Summary:** Exports unaltered store data (including POS transactions) as a CSV for tax authorities.
- **Requirements:** "Compatible for the following countries: Australia, France (Article 88), Germany (GoBD)."
- **Pricing:** Free
- **Verdict:** Only for those three countries.

### 34. Shopify Store Migration
- **Summary:** Import product and customer data from **Square, WooCommerce, Etsy, Wix, Amazon, eBay, Clover and Lightspeed R/X (Vend)**, by CSV upload or an account connection.
- **Pricing:** Free
- **Note from listing:** "Some platforms don't export all data or use compatible formats, so small edits in Shopify may be required."
- **Verdict:** Only when moving to Shopify from one of those platforms.
