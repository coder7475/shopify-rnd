# Naza Noor — Sales Channels Guide

**Last updated:** September 2026

---

## Your Active Sales Channels

### 1. 🌐 Online Store

**URL:** nazanoor.com
**What it is:** Your primary storefront — the main website where customers browse and purchase.

**How to use:**

- Manage products, collections, and pages from **Shopify Admin → Products**
- Customize appearance via **Online Store → Themes**
- Update SEO via each product/collection's "Search engine listing" section
- Manage navigation menus via **Online Store → Navigation**

**Domain & Email Setup References:**
- [Professional Email Setup & Domain Authentication Guide](./professional_email_setup_guide.md) — SPF, DKIM (4 CNAMEs), and DMARC authentication for store emails.
- [Customer Account Subdomain Setup Guide](./customer-url.md) — Setup and DNS verification for `customer-account.nazanoor.com`.

**Best for:** All regular sales, new arrivals, and your primary brand experience.

---

### 2. 🛍️ Shop App

**What it is:** Shopify's free consumer shopping app. Customers who buy from you can track orders and follow your store.

**How to use:**

- Customize your Shop profile via **Sales Channels → Shop**
- Set a store description, featured products, and brand colors
- Customers auto-enroll in order tracking after every purchase — no setup needed
- Use **Shop Pay** to enable faster checkout for returning customers

**Best for:** Post-purchase trust, order tracking, and organic brand discovery.

---

### 3. 💬 Inbox

**What it is:** Shopify's free live chat tool. A chat widget appears on your storefront for customers to message you.

**How to use:**

- Go to **Shopify Inbox** app to view and reply to messages
- Set up **instant answers** for common questions (sizing, shipping, returns)
- Connect to Facebook Messenger if needed
- Respond from the Shopify mobile app on the go

**Best for:** Pre-purchase questions, converting hesitant customers, and customer support.

---

### 4. 🏪 Point of Sale (POS)

**What it is:** Sell in person at events, pop-ups, or markets. Syncs with your online inventory automatically.

**How to use:**

- Download the **Shopify POS app** on a phone or tablet
- Connect a card reader for payments (optional)
- Select products and accept payment (card, cash, Shop Pay)
- Inventory deducts automatically from your online store stock

**Best for:** Cultural events, trade fairs, pop-up markets, and in-person sales.

**Current plan:** POS Lite (free). Upgrade to POS Pro for staff permissions and advanced inventory features.

---

## Quick Reference

| --- | --- | --- |

| Shop App | Free | Order tracking & discovery |
| Inbox | Free | Customer chat & support |
| Point of Sale | Free (Lite) | In-person selling |
