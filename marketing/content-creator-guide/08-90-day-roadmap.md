# Part 8: 90-Day Creator Launch Roadmap

> **Guide Index:** [README](./README.md) | ← [Analytics](./07-analytics.md) | [Checklists →](./09-checklists.md)

---

## Overview

This roadmap gives you a structured 90-day plan to go from zero (or early-stage) to consistently monetizing your content. Each week has specific, achievable tasks with estimated time commitments.

```
Month 1: FOUNDATION      Month 2: CONSISTENCY      Month 3: MONETIZATION
    │                         │                          │
Set up platforms          Post on a schedule         Pitch brand deals
Learn your analytics      Publish first SEO content  Test LIVE selling
Apply to affiliates       Build media kit            Launch Shopify store
Start UTM tracking        Analyze & double down      Quarterly content audit
```

---

## Month 1: Foundation

### Week 1 — Accounts & Tracking

| Task | Time Estimate |
| :--- | :--- |
| Choose your niche and primary platform | 1 hr |
| Create and fully optimize profiles on primary platform (bio, photo, link) | 2 hrs |
| Set up Google Analytics 4 + Google Search Console (if you have a blog) | 2 hrs |
| Submit sitemap.xml in Google Search Console | 30 min |
| Set up a Linktree or bio link page with organized affiliate categories | 1 hr |
| Install UTM tracking on all affiliate links (use a spreadsheet to track) | 1 hr |

**Week 1 Goal:** Every account is live, properly optimized, and tracking is active.

---

### Week 2 — First Content Batch

| Task | Time Estimate |
| :--- | :--- |
| Publish first 5 pieces of content on your primary platform | 2–3 hrs/day |
| Focus on 2 ATTRACT + 2 EDUCATE + 1 CONVERT content pieces | — |
| Apply to TikTok Affiliate Marketplace (if 1K+ followers) | 1 hr |
| Apply to Instagram Creator Marketplace | 30 min |
| Create your first Pinterest board with 10–15 optimized pins | 3 hrs |

**Week 2 Goal:** You have content published, affiliates applied to, and Pinterest started.

---

### Week 3 — Affiliate Foundation

| Task | Time Estimate |
| :--- | :--- |
| Identify and apply to 5–10 brand affiliate programs on TikTok Open Collaboration | 2 hrs |
| Create 1 dedicated affiliate product review video/post | 2–3 hrs |
| Add affiliate product links to all applicable existing content descriptions | 1 hr |
| Cross-post your best Week 2 content to your secondary platform | 1 hr |

**Week 3 Goal:** Your first affiliate content is live with tracked links.

---

### Week 4 — First Analysis

| Task | Time Estimate |
| :--- | :--- |
| Review native analytics: What got the most saves? Most shares? | 1 hr |
| Check affiliate dashboard: Any clicks? Any conversions? | 30 min |
| Note which content format performed best — plan more of it | 30 min |
| Set your posting schedule for Month 2 (be realistic) | 30 min |

**Week 4 Goal:** You know what's working and have a plan for Month 2.

---

## Month 2: Content Consistency

### Week 5 — Cadence & Evergreen Content

| Task | Time Estimate |
| :--- | :--- |
| Lock in your content calendar: 3–5 posts/week on primary, 5–10 pins/week on Pinterest | 1 hr setup |
| Publish your **first blog post or YouTube video** targeting an informational keyword | 4–6 hrs |
| Optimize video/post title, description, and tags for SEO | 1 hr |

**Week 5 Goal:** Your first evergreen SEO content is live.

---

### Week 6 — Brand Outreach

| Task | Time Estimate |
| :--- | :--- |
| Research 10 brands in your niche that fit your audience | 1 hr |
| Reach out to 3 brands for gifting or low-cost affiliate collaboration | 1 hr |
| Reply to all early comments on your content (signals to algorithm) | 30 min/day |

**Week 6 Goal:** Brand outreach is initiated; at least 1 response received.

---

### Week 7 — Media Kit & Comparison Content

| Task | Time Estimate |
| :--- | :--- |
| Build your first media kit (see [Part 5 checklist](./05-monetization.md)) | 3 hrs |
| Publish a **comparison or buying guide** — highest affiliate converters | 4–6 hrs |
| Pin 5 new pins from your latest content | 1 hr |

**Week 7 Goal:** Media kit ready to send; comparison content published.

---

### Week 8 — Affiliate Audit

| Task | Time Estimate |
| :--- | :--- |
| Review affiliate dashboard: Which products converted? Which didn't? | 1 hr |
| Eliminate underperforming affiliate links from bio; promote top converters | 30 min |
| Repurpose your best Month 1 post to a new format or platform | 2 hrs |

**Week 8 Goal:** Your affiliate strategy is focused on what actually converts.

---

## Month 3: Monetization & Scale

### Week 9 — Content Series

| Task | Time Estimate |
| :--- | :--- |
| Identify your top 3 content formats by saves + affiliate conversion | 30 min |
| Plan a 4-part content series around your best-performing topic | 1 hr |
| Publish part 1 of the series with strong internal linking to affiliate products | 2–3 hrs |

**Week 9 Goal:** A content series is underway that builds on your proven formats.

---

### Week 10 — LIVE & Store Launch

| Task | Time Estimate |
| :--- | :--- |
| Test your **first TikTok LIVE selling session** (if applicable, even just 30 minutes) | 1–2 hrs |
| If launching your own store: Install Shopify, connect Meta + TikTok + Pinterest sales channels | 4–6 hrs |
| If launching: Set up GA4 tracking via Google & YouTube Shopify app | 1 hr |

**Week 10 Goal:** First LIVE test complete; store live or in active setup.

---

### Week 11 — Brand Deal Pitch

| Task | Time Estimate |
| :--- | :--- |
| Pitch your first formal brand deal using your media kit (60 days of data to support your ask) | 2 hrs |
| Add 1 FAQ section to your best-performing blog post (boosts AI recommendations) | 1 hr |
| Publish part 3 or 4 of your content series | 2–3 hrs |

**Week 11 Goal:** First brand deal proposal sent; content series near completion.

---

### Week 12 — Quarterly Audit

| Task | Time Estimate |
| :--- | :--- |
| Review top 10 performing posts. Create 3 repurposed versions for other platforms | 3 hrs |
| Run Google Search Console review: new keywords gaining impressions? | 1 hr |
| Update top affiliate posts with fresh links, prices, and data | 1 hr |
| Plan Month 4 content calendar based on learnings | 1 hr |

**Week 12 Goal:** Clear picture of what to double down on; Month 4 planned.

---

## After 90 Days: Where to Focus Next

| Performance Level | Your Next Priority |
| :--- | :--- |
| < 1K followers, < $100 affiliate | Focus on content volume and quality. Post more. Review what gets saves. |
| 1K–5K followers, $100–$500 affiliate/mo | Apply to more targeted brand collaborations. Start building an email list. |
| 5K–25K followers, $500–$2,000/mo | Launch your own Shopify store. Pitch brand deals. Test LIVE selling. |
| 25K+ followers, $2,000+/mo | Scale SEO (blog + YouTube). Run paid ads to top affiliate content. Hire an editor. |
